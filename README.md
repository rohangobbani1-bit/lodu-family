# Lodu Family

An 8-player octagon Ludo game you can play in your phone browser.

## Features
- Play with 2 to 8 players
- Pass N Play, vs Computer, Online and Friends rooms
- Chat, emojis and voice
- Daily rewards and dice colors

## How to play
Open the link, pick a name, and tap PLAY.
